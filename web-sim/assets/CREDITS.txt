  Source: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, "CGI Moon Kit" (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720),
  built from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter data. Public domain in the United States.
  Obtained via Wikimedia Commons (File:Moon_texture.jpg). Downscaled here from
  3840x1920 to 2048x1024 and re-encoded as JPEG (about 0.7 MB).
